# Direct carousel manipulation verification

The featured property carousel now supports a physical left/right pointer drag or touch swipe. A browser-level leftward drag simulation advanced the active listing from **Asteria Residences, 1 of 5** to **The Mews KL, 2 of 5** after the interaction settled.

The carousel retains its button, dot, click-to-open, and keyboard interactions, while the on-screen guidance now communicates drag, swipe, arrow-key, and Enter behavior.

A browser-level touch-style rightward swipe also moved the active listing from **Asteria Residences, 1 of 5** to **Mutiara Ville, 5 of 5**, confirming touch swipe support and seamless wraparound in the reverse direction.

At an emulated **375×812** viewport, a direct leftward mouse drag across the carousel advanced the focused listing from **Mutiara Ville, 5 of 5** to **Asteria Residences, 1 of 5**. This verifies the direct manipulation behavior at the mobile breakpoint as well as the seamless forward wraparound.
