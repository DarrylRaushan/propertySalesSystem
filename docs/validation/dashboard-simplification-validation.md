# Dashboard Simplification Validation

## Desktop

The default dashboard now shows exactly three KPI cards—Expected Sales, Priority Leads, and Active Properties—followed by the action-first Priority Leads workspace and one supporting Sales Trend panel. Total Leads, Pipeline Health, Source Mix, Follow-up Health, and the signal-note card no longer compete in the initial view.

The **Performance details** action opens an on-demand dialog containing Pipeline Health, Source Mix, and Follow-up Health. Each detail retains its own route into the existing deeper interaction, while the default command-center view stays limited to forecast, lead action, and supporting trend context.

## Mobile at 375×812

The simplified mobile sequence presents the headline, period controls, three KPI cards, the five-row priority queue, and the single Sales Trend supporting insight without the previous lower analytics wall. A direct 375×812 check opened **Performance details** and confirmed its dialog fitted the viewport and exposed all three secondary diagnostic panels—Pipeline Health, Source Mix, and Follow-up Health—without horizontal overflow.
